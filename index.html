Exness Personal Area Login vs. MT4 and MT5 Login

One common source of confusion among new users is the difference between Personal Area login and trading-platform login.

The Personal Area is used for general account management. It normally requires the registered email address and Personal Area password.

MetaTrader 4 (MT4) and MetaTrader 5 (MT5), where supported for a particular account, use trading-account details instead. These may include a trading account number, trading password, and the correct server.
The Personal Area password and trading password should not be assumed to be interchangeable. Users can find their trading account number and server details in the relevant account information section of their Personal Area.

How to Reset a Forgotten Exness Password

Forgetting a password can prevent users from accessing their accounts. Fortunately, Exness provides a password-recovery process for the Personal Area.

If you cannot remember your login password, follow these steps:
Open the official Exness Personal Area login page.
Select the I forgot my password option.
Enter the email address registered with your account.
Continue with the recovery instructions.
Complete the security verification required for your account.
Create a new password that meets the displayed requirements.

Choose a password that is difficult to guess and has not been used on another website.

Exness distinguishes between Personal Area passwords, trading passwords, and read-only access passwords. If you are unable to log in to MT4 or MT5, you may need to reset the trading password for the relevant trading account rather than changing the Personal Area password.

Never share password-reset codes or verification messages with another person.

Common Exness Login Problems and Their Solutions

Sometimes users may experience login difficulties even when they believe their account information is correct. Understanding common causes can help resolve these problems.

Incorrect Email Address:
The Personal Area username is the registered email address. Entering a trading account number or an incorrect email address can result in a failed login.
Check the spelling of your email address and make sure there are no additional spaces.

Incorrect Password:
Passwords are case-sensitive. Check for typing errors, capitalization mistakes, or an incorrect keyboard language.
If you are unsure of the password, use the official recovery process instead of repeatedly guessing.

Verification Code Problems:
Some accounts require additional security verification. If you do not receive a verification code, check your registered email or phone, including spam and junk folders.
Make sure your registered contact details are still accessible. Do not share verification codes with anyone.

Browser Issues:
An outdated browser or stored website data may sometimes interfere with login.
You can try:
Clearing your browser cache and cookies.
Updating your browser.
Opening the website in a private or incognito window.
Trying another supported browser.
Checking your internet connection.
Account Restrictions or Termination

Some account-access problems may relate to account status rather than incorrect login details. For example, a terminated Personal Area may not be recoverable.

If the issue continues, consult the official Exness Help Center or contact support through the verified website.
